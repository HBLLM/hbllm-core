## 1. Glossary & Key Definitions
* **Lever**: A rigid bar pivoted at a fixed point called the fulcrum used to transmit and multiply applied mechanical force.
* **Fulcrum**: The fixed physical pivot point about which a lever rotates or pivots.
* **Stick**: An elongated rigid tool body acting as a lever arm to reach and manipulate distant objects.
* **Box**: A rigid enclosed volume or container capable of supporting and storing physical loads.
* **Pull**: An action applying tensile directional force along a lever or reach tool towards the pivot.
* **Mechanical Advantage**: The ratio of the output force magnitude exerted on a load to the input force applied by the operator.

## 2. Worked Problem: Indirect Manipulation of Loads using Levers and Reach Tools
In physical mechanics, when a target load or object is out of direct physical reach of an operator, a simple machine or rigid lever arm is employed.
* Instruction: `pull green ball inside box`
* Physical Scenario: An object of interest (mass 0.5kg) is located at position (1.2, 0.4), exceeding the direct reach radius of the manipulator. A rigid reach stick (length 0.8m, mass 1.0kg) is located on the table at (0.3, 0.1). A yellow storage container is located at (0.0, 0.6).
* Mechanics Execution: The operator grasps the rigid tool, extends its distal lever arm to contact the distant load, applies tensile force to pull the object within proximal reach, grasps the object, and places it inside the storage container.

## 3. Relational Analogy: Tabletop Lever Systems to Industrial Ore Hoppers and Hydraulic Cranes
The mechanical and structural conservation laws governing simple tabletop tools apply symmetrically across macroscopic industrial systems.
* Source Domain: Tabletop Lever and Container System (input force applied through tool arm into open container).
* Target Domain: Industrial Ore Hopper and Hydraulic Manipulator (high-capacity ore discharge bin with 5000L steel containment).
* Structural Mapping: The physical properties of containment, directed force transmission, and payload transport map symmetrically without altering governing physical relations.
