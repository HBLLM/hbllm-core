## 1. Glossary & Key Definitions
* **Combustion**: A rapid chemical reaction accompanied by the production of heat, light, and gaseous products.
* **Wax**: A solid hydrocarbon fuel that liquefies upon heating and rises through capillary action.
* **Capillarity**: The attraction between liquids and solids causing liquid wax to rise against gravity in the wick.
* **Stick**: A solid rigid taper or fuel rod used to transmit heat and initiate ignition.
* **Box**: A glass bell jar or containment lantern providing physical boundaries to isolate combustion gases.
* **Pull**: Directing an air draught or suction force to channel combustion products through an analysis tube.
